Clinical Standards

Uncompromising Compliance & Security

Your health data and specimen integrity are treated with the same rigor as a hospital lab — delivered to your door.

HIPAA Privacy Standards

All patient health information is handled under strict HIPAA regulations. Your personal data is never shared, stored unsecurely, or accessed beyond clinical necessity.

CLIA-Aligned Protocols

Specimen collection, labeling, and transport follow CLIA-aligned standards — ensuring accuracy and integrity from draw to processing lab.

Certified & Insured Phlebotomists

Every technician is nationally certified and carries professional liability insurance. Credentials are verified and maintained continuously.

Chain of Custody Integrity

From collection through transport, every specimen is tracked and preserved — cold chain protocols maintained for temperature-sensitive panels.
